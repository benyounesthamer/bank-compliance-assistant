# Delta Bank — Customer Complaints Policy

Fictitious document, written to test the tool.

## 1. Scope

Delta Bank's complaint-handling framework is part of its policy for protecting the interests and personal data of banking customers.

## 2. Organisation

Complaints are handled by the Customer Care Unit, whose size is set according to the bank's size, its activities and the yearly volume of complaints. The organisation is considered adequate by the Executive Committee. The Unit's powers are defined in its charter; it has eleven staff and the human, logistical and technical resources it needs, and access to all data and documents required for its work, in line with personal-data protection rules and professional secrecy.

## 3. Procedures

Written internal procedures and internal-control rules govern how complaints are received, handled and archived. They are reviewed every year.

## 4. Channels

Customers can file a complaint by e-mail, through the online form on our website, or in person at any branch or at head office.

## 5. Informing customers

The complaint procedures, handling times and the conditions for referring a case to the banking mediator are shown on posters in every branch, on our website and mobile app, on account statements, and in our marketing, pre-contractual and contractual documents.

## 6. Handling

Every complaint receives an acknowledgement of receipt stating its registration date and a reference number.

Each complaint is examined carefully and answered in writing within fifteen (15) calendar days of the acknowledgement of receipt.

Any answer that rejects all or part of a customer's request states the reasons for the decision.

## 7. Tooling

A dedicated complaints application stores and archives every complaint in a database, by subject and outcome, issues an automatic numbered acknowledgement for complaints received remotely, and alerts the Customer Care Unit when a handling deadline is missed.

## 8. Governance

Each year, the Executive Committee submits a report on complaint handling to the Board of Directors.

Internal Audit reviews the complaint-handling framework at least every three years and whenever needed; the Audit Committee reviews and approves the resulting report.

## 9. Correspondent

The head of the Customer Care Unit is the bank's correspondent for the Central Bank of Tunisia and for the banking mediator, and provides them with any information or documents they request on complaint handling.
