# Bank Compliance Assistant

Check a bank's internal policy against Tunisian central-bank circular 2022-08, or ask a question about 22 circulars in French, English or Arabic and get an answer with its sources.

- **Gap analysis:** load one of the four fictional example policies (instant, from saved results) or paste your own (live, limited to a few requests per hour on this public demo).
- **Questions:** answers cite the circular, article and page, or say the circulars do not contain the answer.

Source code, method and evaluation: https://github.com/benyounesthamer/bank-compliance-assistant

Not legal advice. The example policies are fictional.
